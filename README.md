## 👨‍💻 About Me

I am a passionate **AI Engineer, Systems Architect, and BCA Student** based in Gandhinagar, Gujarat. As the Founder & CEO of **[Mansitra](https://www.mansitra.in)**, I build production-grade AI platforms designed to democratize mental wellness, optimize healthcare clinic workflows, and engineer high-performance software systems.

- 🏥 **HealthTech & Clinical Automation:** Architecting [MediQ OPD](https://smart-opd-queue-tracker.web.app) with sub-10ms real-time queue synchronization, 15-minute generic medicine matching, and DPDP-compliant consent workflows.
- 🧠 **Multimodal & Generative AI:** Engineering frame-by-frame attention scoring engines ([VidPulse AI](https://github.com/yashpatadiya1724-cmyk/vidpulse-ai)) and interactive multi-agent historical personality simulations ([History AI Roleplay](https://github.com/yashpatadiya1724-cmyk/history-ai-roleplay)).
- 🎮 **Game Systems & Graphics:** Authoring modular arcade engines ([Super Game Hub](https://github.com/yashpatadiya1724-cmyk/super-game-hub)) with 40+ playable games, trauma-based screen shake physics (`flutter_game_juice`), and Finite State Machine AI (`fsm_game_ai`).
- 📦 **Containerized Deployments:** Continuous publishing of production multi-stage microservices directly to GitHub Container Registry (`ghcr.io`).

## 🚀 Flagship Projects

### 🏥 Healthcare & Mental Wellness
- **[Mansitra](https://github.com/yashpatadiya1724-cmyk/mansitra_APP)** — *Friend of the Mind*  
  Multilingual AI emotional companion and mental wellness ecosystem for students. Built with React, TypeScript, Groq LLaMA 3.3, Supabase, and Tailwind CSS.  
  🔗 [Live App](https://www.mansitra.in) · [Code](https://github.com/yashpatadiya1724-cmyk/mansitra_APP)
- **[MediQ OPD](https://github.com/yashpatadiya1724-cmyk/OPD)** — *Smart Clinic & Pharmacy Network*  
  Real-time hospital token board, 15-minute hyperlocal Jan Aushadhi generic medicine cost-savings calculator, and DPDP Act 2023 digital consent architecture.  
  🔗 [Live Dashboard](https://smart-opd-queue-tracker.web.app) · [Code](https://github.com/yashpatadiya1724-cmyk/OPD)
- **[MedGenius AI](https://github.com/yashpatadiya1724-cmyk/MedGenius-AI)** — *AI Drug Discovery & Treatment Navigator*  
  NLP-driven drug discovery engine analyzing side effects and interaction graphs for 10,000+ clinical datasets.  
  🔗 [Code](https://github.com/yashpatadiya1724-cmyk/MedGenius-AI)

### 🤖 Generative & Agentic AI
- **[VidPulse AI](https://github.com/yashpatadiya1724-cmyk/vidpulse-ai)** — *Video Retention & Hook Scoring Engine*  
  Frame-by-frame OpenCV computer vision engine analyzing 3-second hook drop-off, visual saliency, and pacing virality for creators.  
  🔗 [Code](https://github.com/yashpatadiya1724-cmyk/vidpulse-ai) · [GHCR Package](https://github.com/yashpatadiya1724-cmyk/vidpulse-ai/pkgs/container/vidpulse-ai)
- **[History AI Roleplay](https://github.com/yashpatadiya1724-cmyk/history-ai-roleplay)** — *Multi-Agent Historical Personality Simulator*  
  FastAPI and ChromaDB RAG backend allowing interactive live debates and dialogue with historical icons like Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj, Albert Einstein, and Mahatma Gandhi.  
  🔗 [Code](https://github.com/yashpatadiya1724-cmyk/history-ai-roleplay) · [GHCR Package](https://github.com/yashpatadiya1724-cmyk/history-ai-roleplay/pkgs/container/history-ai-roleplay)
- **[Dharohar 4D](https://github.com/yashpatadiya1724-cmyk/dharohar-4d)** — *Heritage Neural Digital Twin*  
  Autonomous AI neural 3D digital twin and ancient epigraphy restoration engine for Indian cultural heritage monuments (IIT Gandhinagar / Blithchron).  
  🔗 [Code](https://github.com/yashpatadiya1724-cmyk/dharohar-4d)

### 🎮 Game Engines & Graphics Systems
- **[Super Game Hub](https://github.com/yashpatadiya1724-cmyk/super-game-hub)** — *Cross-Platform 40+ Game Arcade Suite*  
  High-performance Flutter game hub featuring 40+ playable retro and puzzle classics, custom particle systems, trauma screen shake, 3-level campaign progression, and reusable packages.
